About • Overview

Deep Technical Expertise from Microarchitecture to Silicon

Bridging the gap between cutting-edge processor microarchitecture, non-blocking NoCs, and silicon tape-out verification methodologies.

Hardware Architect & Verification Leader

With over 4.5+ years of production semiconductor engineering experience, my work is centered on high-performance compute architectures: RISC-V SoC integration, non-blocking AMBA AXI4 crossbars, hardware-enforced cache coherence protocols, and robust Memory controller subsystems.

I served as the technical lead for a quad-core RV64GV SoC targeting 1.5 GHz on TSMC 12nm, stewarding the architecture, peripheral NoC partitioning, and comprehensive assembly/C-based system verification through tape-out with a 30+ engineer cross-functional team for a California-based fabless semiconductor client.

Currently at ADN Semiconductors, I direct the microarchitecture and AXI integration for custom Memory controller IP with a ~50-engineer team. Beyond commercial chips, as Founder & Maintainer of Squared Studio, I build and curate open-source SystemVerilog hardware primitives, educating the next generation of silicon architects.
